The answer we’re looking for

M12 − S12 is the primary net-return comparison. MMIX − SMIX is a within-study robustness check using the blended signal, not independent replication. Their difference measures the secondary interaction.

The context that matters

Actual SPMO and SPY provide real-world benchmarks. These custom portfolios do not replicate the official index. Return, drawdown, turnover, and costs must travel together.

A note on evidence

The 2026 YTD window runs from December 31, 2025 close to October 2, 2026 close, after a 2025 portfolio burn-in. Those market moves already informed the idea, so this is a retrospective case study. Longer history across different market conditions is planned, subject to data acceptance; the supported period must be frozen before comparing results.

Calendar sensitivity

Could the result depend on the months chosen? Six calendar schedules, all reported; synthetic validation only. The market-data check will compare all six twice-yearly schedules, six months apart. The primary schedule remains March/September; no best-performing calendar will replace it. Read the calendar protocol or inspect the synthetic receipt.
