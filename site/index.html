New / fixed-cohort stock pilot

A good year is
not the whole story.

At 5 bps per side, monthly 12–1 selection led the semiannual portfolio by 8.84 percentage points in 2026 through October 2. It lagged by 6.93 points in 2025. Across both periods the difference was just 1.23 points, and reversed at higher costs.

Read the scope

The 100 largest baseline issuers supply a fixed starting cohort, with 20 target positions and one security class per issuer. Sharadar supplies issuer metrics; Yahoo supplies adjusted-price proxies. The answer we’re looking for

M12 − S12 is the primary net-return comparison. MMIX − SMIX is a within-study robustness check using the blended signal, not independent replication. Their difference measures the secondary interaction.

The context that matters

Actual SPMO and SPY provide real-world benchmarks. These custom portfolios do not replicate the official index. Return, drawdown, turnover, and costs must travel together.

A note on evidence

For the original stock study, the proposed 2026 YTD window runs from December 31, 2025 close to October 2, 2026 close, after a 2025 portfolio burn-in. Those market moves already informed the idea, so this is a retrospective case study. Longer history across different market conditions is planned, subject to data acceptance; the supported period must be frozen before comparing results.

Calendar sensitivity

Could the result depend on the months chosen? For the original stock model: six calendar schedules, all reported; synthetic validation only. The market-data check will compare all six twice-yearly schedules, six months apart. The primary schedule remains March/September; no best-performing calendar will replace it. Read the calendar protocol or inspect the synthetic receipt.
