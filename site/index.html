The answer we’re looking for

M12 − S12 is the primary net-return comparison. MMIX − SMIX checks whether the frequency effect also appears with the blended signal. Their difference measures the secondary interaction.

The context that matters

Actual SPMO and SPY provide real-world benchmarks. These custom portfolios do not replicate the official index. Return, drawdown, turnover, and costs must travel together.

A note on evidence

The 2026 YTD window runs from December 31, 2025 close to October 2, 2026 close, after a 2025 portfolio burn-in. Those market moves already informed the idea, so it remains historical exploration. Forward evidence begins only after the rules are frozen.

02 / The data

The hard part isn’t the code.
It’s knowing what was knowable.

Today’s stock list cannot stand in for yesterday’s universe. A credible test needs historical membership, prices, security-level market caps, and corporate actions.

Source plan · not connected

These are candidate routes, not approved datasets. Coverage, timestamps, identifiers, dividends, delistings, and licensing must pass review before any route powers a research result.
