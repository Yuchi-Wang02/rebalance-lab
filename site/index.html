Observed variation
in performance.

The relative performance of monthly and semiannual rebalancing varied by period, trading cost, and portfolio specification. The results do not establish a general advantage for more frequent rebalancing.

01

Period sensitivity. Monthly stock selection led in 2026 YTD after trailing in 2025.

02

Cost sensitivity. The stock pilot’s small full-period advantage disappeared at higher trading costs.

03

Risk and return. Monthly sector-ETF selection had a shallower drawdown and lower long-period growth.

Retrospective simulations subject to model and data limitations. No predictive or universal performance claim.

Empirical design

Rebalancing frequency.
Matched comparisons.

Momentum strategies select assets based on past performance. The primary comparison changes the frequency of portfolio selection.

01

Controlled strategy comparisons.

Within each primary pair, the universe, signal and portfolio rules remain fixed. Selection occurs monthly or semiannually.

02

Transaction-cost sensitivity.

Costs apply to each purchase and sale. Scenarios of 0, 5, 10 and 25 bps per side are reported alongside return and drawdown.

03

Complete comparative reporting.

Reported comparisons cover prespecified periods, calendar schedules and signal variants. A favorable year does not replace the complete results.
