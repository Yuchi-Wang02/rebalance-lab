02 / Where the result comes from

Real inputs.
A deliberately limited model.

01

A frozen starting cohort

The baseline ranks issuers by Sharadar DAILY capitalization at December 31, 2024, and retains 100 issuers with one covered security class each. The cohort stays fixed. Later additions to the wider index do not expand it.

This tests a restricted starting universe, not the full evolving S&P 500.

02

Four arms, 20 target holdings

Each arm selects positive-momentum candidates with the same portfolio rules. S12 and M12 use 12–1 momentum. SMIX and MMIX use the 50/30/20 blend. Selection is monthly or at March/September month-end; orders use the next session.

Cost scenarios: 0, 5, 10 and 25 bps per side. The blended pair is a prespecified replication.

03

Dated data, with limits

Purchased Sharadar SF1, DAILY, SP500 and ACTIONS components support the data work; Yahoo supplies free price proxies. Vendor dates do not prove original publication-time availability, and adjusted prices do not reproduce a raw-share corporate-action ledger.

No dividend cash is added a second time to the adjusted accounting units. Raw vendor observations are not published here.

04

Holdings cross the year-end

Portfolios form after the December 31, 2024 signal and run through 2025. The 2026 view starts with their existing holdings at December 31, 2025 close, and ends October 2, 2026. Changing the displayed window does not reset the simulated strategy.

This history was already observable. It remains retrospective exploration, not a new out-of-sample test.
